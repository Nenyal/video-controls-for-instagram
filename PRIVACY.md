# Privacy policy

This extension does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data.

**What it does on your device**

- It runs only on `www.instagram.com`, to add video controls and download buttons to the page.
- To download media, it requests the media's file information from Instagram's own web API, using the
  session you're already signed in with, the same way instagram.com does. These requests go only to
  Instagram.
- Files you choose to download are saved to your browser's downloads folder.
- Your settings (playback speed, volume, shortcuts and display options) are stored with Chrome's
  `chrome.storage.sync`, which your browser may sync between your own devices.

**What it does not do**

- It has no server and sends nothing to its developer or any third party.
- It contains no analytics, tracking, advertising or remote code.
- It does not read or store your messages, account details or browsing history.

Questions: open an issue on the project's repository.
